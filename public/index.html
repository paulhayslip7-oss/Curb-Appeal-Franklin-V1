About us

In Franklin Since 1999.

I grew up in Founders Pointe, back before Westhaven was even an idea. I've watched Franklin grow into the community it is today, and I'm proud to call it home.

I'm married to my high school sweetheart, Britney, a physician assistant in dermatology. Six years into marriage, our three-year-old Bernese Mountain Dog keeps us plenty busy.

I spent eight years in the lawn care industry, building and eventually selling my first company, Hayslip Lawn Care. I traded the mower for a desk, thinking corporate America was my future. Nearly a decade in business development taught me a lot about what customers actually value: reliability, professionalism, clear communication, and a company that delivers on its promises.

Now, I'm bringing that experience back to the industry where it all started.

Exterior maintenance should always be proactive instead of reactive.

Our approach is simple: exceptional attention to detail, proactive communication, consistent quality, and a property that looks its best year-round. No chasing down your lawn guy, no wondering when someone's showing up, and no settling for work that's just good enough.

We believe your time is valuable, your home is worth taking pride in, and great service should be something you can count on.
