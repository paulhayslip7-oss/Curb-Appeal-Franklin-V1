About us

In Franklin Since 1999.

I grew up in Franklin and have watched it grow into the community it is today. I'm proud to still call it home.

I'm married to my high school sweetheart, Britney, a physician assistant in dermatology. Seven years in, our three-year-old Bernese Mountain Dog keeps us busy.

I spent 10 years in lawn care and built and sold my first company, Hayslip Lawn Care. Nearly a decade in corporate business development then taught me what customers value most: reliability, clear communication and promises kept.

Now I'm bringing that experience back to where it started.
